# Coop Okinawa (コープおきなわ) and Okinawan foods for a weaning shopping list

## Key findings (read first)
- **Order timing is the main design constraint.** Coop Okinawa delivers once a week, on a fixed weekday. You order this week and the goods arrive **7 days later**. In the monthly baby catalogue (すくすくスマイル), items from other makers arrive **14 days** after the order; only CO-OP-brand items arrive the next week. So a "next week" list is due at this week's order deadline, and some baby items need two weeks' notice.
- **The baby-food proof is from June 2026 catalogues.** Coop Okinawa publishes a sample of its baby catalogue, すくすくスマイル 2026年6月号 (issues 610–613). It is an Okinawa-specific edition: the file is named `sukusuku-oki` and it has a Coop Okinawa delivery calendar. I also read the first 10 pages of the weekly あっぷる catalogue, issue 611 (6月2週). I found no September catalogue. The product range rotates: each item shows "次回予定 X月Y週" (next planned week), and only items marked 毎週 appear every week.
- **Where I found no source, I say so.** No Okinawan public body gives a weaning stage for ゴーヤー, ナーベーラー, ハンダマ, フーチバー, グルクン, 青パパイヤ, パッションフルーツ or シークヮーサー. For those the stage comes from the food's category or from commercial dietitian sites, and confidence is low.
- **A published Okinawan food chart exists.** The 沖縄県小児保健協会 weaning leaflet (edited by 沖縄県栄養士会, 2020) introduces 沖縄豆腐 (島豆腐) at 中期, 海そう at 中期 and 沖縄そば at 完了期.

## 1. How Coop Okinawa works

**Store vs home delivery**
- **Stores:** 9 in total. Naha has 5 (あっぷるタウン, おろく, こくば, 首里, 寒川), Okinawa City 2 (美里, 山内), Urasoe 1 (牧港) and Nago 1 (なご宮里). I could not confirm whether stores stock きらきらステップ; articles say store stock varies by co-op, store and season. Confidence: high for the store list, low for store stock. Sources: [okinawa.coop/shops](https://www.okinawa.coop/shops/), [Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/生活協同組合コープおきなわ), [baby.omosuku.co.jp](https://baby.omosuku.co.jp/coop-babyfood-shop/)
- **Home delivery is called 協同購入**, with four ways to receive it. Confidence: medium-high. Source: [okinawa.coop/deli](https://www.okinawa.coop/deli/)

| Option | How it works | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 個別利用 | Delivered to your door | ¥250 per delivery, discounted for larger orders |
| ごいっしょ個配 | Two households share one drop-off | ¥125 each |
| グループ利用 | Neighbourhood group drop-off | No fee |
| ステーション利用 | Pick-up point | No fee |

- Another site says the fee is halved from ¥3,000, free from ¥12,000, and free for the first 4 weeks. I only saw this in a search summary because the page was under maintenance. Confidence: medium-low. Source: [coop-takuhai.jp](https://www.coop-takuhai.jp/takuhai/search/pref/okinawa/okinawa_coopokinawa/system)
- **Delivery days:** Monday to Friday, public holidays included, no weekends. Islands not linked to the main island by bridge pay a 離島手数料 (island surcharge). Confidence: high. Source: [okinawa.coop/faq/delivery](https://www.okinawa.coop/faq/delivery/)
- Some catalogue items cannot be ordered by members served from the Miyako, Ishigaki or Kume centres (notice on あっぷる 611号 p.10). Confidence: medium.

**Weekly cycle**
- **あっぷる (weekly food catalogue):**
  - Example, issue 611: order form handed in 6/1–5 (week A), goods delivered 6/8–12 (week B), same weekday. The catalogue itself arrives with the previous week's goods.
  - In practice: catalogue arrives → order at your next delivery → goods one week later. Confidence: high. Source: [あっぷる 611号 cover](https://coop.okinawa/assets/pdf/apple_all_v3.pdf)
- **Phone or fax orders:** until 19:00 the day after your delivery day. If your delivery day is Friday, the deadline is 20:00 the same day. Confidence: high. Source: [FAQ](https://www.okinawa.coop/faq/delivery/)
- **すくすくスマイル (monthly baby catalogue, free by registration, marked 保存版):**
  - Its calendar shows orders in week N delivered in week N+2. Example: issue 610, ordered 5/25–29, delivered 6/8–12.
  - Items with the red co-op mark are "注文日の翌週にお届け" (delivered the week after the order). Confidence: high (read directly from the calendar).
  - Coop Kyushu confirms the general rule: "注文締切日により翌週または翌々週のお届け" (next week or the week after, depending on the deadline). Source: [CO・OP+ FAQ](https://www.kyushu.coop/coopkyushu/coopplus/)
- **いつもくん (automatic repeat delivery):** you register items and they are delivered automatically. Only items from 保存版 catalogues qualify, and すくすくスマイル is one. It suits staples like おかゆ, うどん and vegetable cubes. Confidence: high for eligibility; the delivery frequency is not stated. Source: [okinawa.coop/deli/itsumokun](https://www.okinawa.coop/deli/itsumokun/)

**Ordering online**
- **eフレンズ九州** is the web ordering site. It needs its own registration.
  - PC: https://kyushu.ef.cws.coop/auth/bb/login.do
  - Smartphone: https://kyushu.ef.cws.coop/auth/sp/login.do
  - Coop Okinawa advertises a later deadline than paper forms ("注文締切時間が長い"). You can change the order as often as you like until then.
  - The exact deadline appears on each week's shopping screen; I could not find it published for Okinawa. Confidence: high for the service, unknown for the exact deadline. Sources: [okinawa.coop/deli/weborder](https://www.okinawa.coop/deli/weborder/), [coopkyushu.info Q&A](https://coopkyushu.info/efg/qanda/index.html)
- **CO・OP+ (コーププラス)** is Coop Kyushu's app. It covers membership points and ordering by item number, and has Okinawa-specific notes about points. It is not a full catalogue app. Confidence: medium. Source: [kyushu.coop/coopkyushu/coopplus](https://www.kyushu.coop/coopkyushu/coopplus/)
- **Allergen and origin lookup:** JCCU's database for CO-OP-brand products, linked from Coop Okinawa: https://mdinfo.jccu.coop/bb/

**What this means for the app**
- The list must be final before this week's deadline: your delivery day for paper, 19:00 the next day for phone, or the eフレンズ deadline.
- Items from other makers in すくすくスマイル need a two-week horizon.
- Some items are not in every week's catalogue, so the app should offer a store or fresh-market fallback.

**Cautions**
- Plain frozen vegetables usually contain no salt, but check the 食塩相当量 (salt) line on the pack.
- Ordinary しらす / 釜あげしらす need desalting (soak in or pour over boiling water for a few minutes, then drain). Salt-free しらす does not. Source: [母子栄養協会](https://boshieiyou.org/shirasushionuki/)
- Frozen white fish may still contain bones; check before serving.
- "8品目無" does not mean soy-free: 納豆ペースト, the 豆乳 sauces and 枝豆 contain 大豆.
- Frozen berries: heat before serving to an infant. This is standard food-safety practice, not something I found sourced here. Confidence: low.

## 4. Okinawan local produce and fish

**Stage and season sources**
- Stage rule from the national guide ([授乳・離乳の支援ガイド 2019 (national weaning guide)](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/11908000/000496257.pdf), p.32–34): start with おかゆ, then vegetables, then 豆腐 / 白身魚 / 卵黄. Fish goes white → red → blue-backed. Seaweed is added as weaning progresses. No seasoning at the start.
- Stages specific to Okinawa: the [沖縄県小児保健協会 leaflet](https://www.town.yonabaru.okinawa.jp/uploaded/attachment/708.pdf).
- Harvest seasons:
  - [沖縄元気野菜カレンダー](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/cms/wp-content/themes/kuwachii/img/agri/calendar.pdf), from くゎっちーおきなわ, the prefecture's farm-and-fisheries information site
  - [JAおきなわ 生産カレンダー](https://www.ja-okinawa.or.jp/cast/calendar/calendar/)

**Vegetables**
- **島にんじん (yellow island carrot):** 初期, as a carrot. Harvest Oct–Mar, peak Nov–Feb. Peel and cook very soft. Confidence: high for stage, high for season.
- **島かぼちゃ:** 初期. Harvest Oct–Jun (peak Jun and Oct–Dec); **none Jul–Sep**, so in September use CO-OP うらごしかぼちゃ or mainland pumpkin. Watery, mild taste. Source: [kuwachii 2543](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/agricultural/2543/). Confidence: high.
- **紅いも:** 初期. The site gives its Japanese name as さつまいも (sweet potato). Harvest Aug–Jan. Source: [kuwachii 2564](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/agricultural/2564/). Confidence: high.
  - **Do not confuse it with ヤマン / 紅山芋 (a yam, ダイジョ).** Yam counts as やまいも (a recommended-label allergen), makes the mouth itch and is 後期+ ([mynavi](https://kosodate.mynavi.jp/articles/8382)). Confidence: medium.
- **田芋 (ターンム):** a taro (サトイモ科), so treat like 里芋: **中期〜**, not 初期, because it is sticky and can make the mouth itch ([mynavi 里芋](https://kosodate.mynavi.jp/articles/8368)). Season Dec–Apr. Avoid sweetened dishes such as 田芋 きんとん and ドゥルワカシー. Source: [kuwachii 田芋](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/manga/4456/). Confidence: medium.
- **シブイ (冬瓜):** 初期〜中期. Remove skin and seeds; it is watery and mild. Peak May–Sep, available all year. Source: [mynavi 冬瓜](https://kosodate.mynavi.jp/articles/9383). Confidence: medium-high.
- **ナーベーラー (へちま):** no authoritative source. Soft, mild and slightly slippery; treat like a gourd, 中期〜, peeled and cooked. Peak May–Sep. Source: only one dietitian-supervised 後期 recipe ([baby-calendar](https://baby-calendar.jp/baby-food-recipe/recipe/5623)). Confidence: low.
- **ゴーヤー:** sources disagree (roughly 後期 to 1歳6か月) and none is authoritative. Advice: **leave it out of weaning.** At 完了期, try a small amount after salting and blanching. Available all year, peak Apr–Jul. Sources: [tomonite recipe 1歳6ヶ月〜](https://tomonite.com/articles/5958), [baby-koha](https://baby-koha.com/archives/2051). Confidence: low.
- **ハンダマ (スイゼンジナ):** no source on infants. If used, leaves only, cooked and finely chopped, 後期〜. Peak Nov–May. Confidence: low.
- **フーチバー (ヨモギ):** bitter, strong aroma, no infant source. Not needed during weaning. Confidence: low.
- **青パパイヤ (野菜パパイヤ):** papain can cause allergy, cross-reacts with latex allergy, and large amounts can cause diarrhoea ([kumitasu](https://www.kumitasu.com/contents/hyoji/923)). If used, 後期〜, well cooked, small amounts. Peak Jul–Sep. Confidence: low.

**Seaweed**
- **アーサ (ヒトエグサ):** seaweed, so 中期 per the Okinawa leaflet. It sticks to the throat, so chop finely and cook in soup. Rinse out sand. Keep amounts small because of iodine. Season Jan–Apr. Sources: [kuwachii アーサ](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/agricultural/7650/), [琉球新報](https://ryukyushimpo.jp/news/region/entry-4010272.html). Confidence: medium.
- **もずく:** seaweed, but its long slippery strands mean most sources say **後期**, finely chopped ([tomonite](https://tomonite.com/articles/4573), [KIDSNA](https://kidsna.com/magazine/article/lifestyle-recipe-190808-00008182)).
  - Salted もずく needs thorough desalting.
  - **Seasoned もずく (味付け, in vinegar) is not suitable.**
  - Harvest Apr–Jun; sold salted or frozen all year ([kuwachii 4869](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/agricultural/4869/)). Confidence: medium.

**Tofu**
- **島豆腐 (沖縄豆腐):** the Okinawa leaflet puts 沖縄豆腐 at **中期**, with 絹ごし豆腐 at 初期. Confidence: high.
  - **Salt 0.4g/100g** (木綿豆腐: 0g); protein 9.1g/100g ([食品成分DB](https://fooddb.mext.go.jp/details/details.pl?ITEM_NO=4_04036_7)).
  - A 中期 portion of 30–40g carries about 0.12–0.16g salt. Blanch it and mash well because it is firm.
  - Allergen: 大豆.
- **ゆし豆腐:** 0.6g salt/100g ([DB](https://fooddb.mext.go.jp/details/details.pl?ITEM_NO=4_04037_7)). Drain off the liquid; 中期〜. Confidence: medium.
- **ジーマーミ豆腐: contains 落花生 (peanut), a mandatory allergen.** Tomigusuku City and the Okinawa tourism site (おきなわ物語) warn of rising anaphylaxis cases, "特に小さなお子様の事例が増えております" (especially in young children) ([豊見城市](https://www.city.tomigusuku.lg.jp/soshiki/3/1017/gyomuannai/1/6461.html), [おきなわ物語](https://www.okinawastory.jp/news/notice/4239)). It is also starch-based and sticky, usually served with a sweet sauce. **Keep it off the weaning list**; introduce peanut separately, on its own terms. Confidence: high.

**Fish**
- **グルクン (タカサゴ):** year-round ([kuwachii 7654](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/agricultural/7654/)). No infant source; mild fish, so 白身魚 from 初期 late or 中期. Many small bones; buy fresh rather than the common fried versions. Confidence: medium-low.
- **ミーバイ (ハタ):** white, delicate flesh, year-round, so 白身魚 初期〜. **Ciguatera warning:** バラハタ (ナガジューミーバイ) is a known toxic species in Okinawa, and you cannot tell a toxic fish by look or taste. The prefecture advises not eating fish when species or size is uncertain; buy only identified fish from a retailer ([沖縄県 シガテラ](https://www.pref.okinawa.jp/kurashikankyo/shoku/1004101/1018584/1004114.html)). Confidence: medium.
- **マグロ / カツオ:** red fish, 中期 (the leaflet says "8か月頃から赤身魚"). Fresh tuna is landed year-round ([kuwachii 4878](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/agricultural/4878/)). Use only very fresh fish or canned tuna with no salt and no oil. Confidence: high.

**Fruit**
- **島バナナ:** banana, 初期 (heat it at first). Allergen: バナナ. Season Jun–Oct, scarce ([kuwachii 7658](https://kuwachii-okinawa.com/agricultural/7658/)). Regular bananas are in あっぷる every week (#36). Confidence: high.
- **マンゴー:** sources conflict. It is in the same plant family as poison ivy (ウルシ科) and can cause rashes around the mouth; one dietitian site (tomonite) says only after 1歳, in small amounts ([search summary](https://tomonite.com/articles/3718)). JA season is late Jun to mid-Oct. Confidence: low-medium.
- **シークヮーサー:** very acidic. Use a few diluted drops as flavouring from 後期 at the earliest. Season: green-harvest Aug–early Oct (peak Sep), ripe Jan–Feb (JA). Confidence: low.
- **パッションフルーツ:** strain out the seeds; not needed during weaning. JA season Feb–Jul. Confidence: low.

**Noodles**
- **沖縄そば:** 完了期 per the leaflet. Contains 小麦 and salt. Rinse the noodles in hot water and never use the seasoned broth. Confidence: high for the stage.

## Weekly quantities per stage

Per-meal amounts are from the national 2019 guide, p.34 ("1回当たりの目安量"). They are guidelines to adjust to the baby's appetite. The protein foods are **alternatives ("又は")**: one portion per meal. If a meal combines two protein foods, halve each.

- **初期 (5–6 months):** 1 meal a day, 2 a day after about one month; the national guide gives no gram amounts.
  - By Naha's schedule, the end of month one is about 30g つぶしがゆ, about 15g vegetables and 5–10g protein per meal.
  - Per week (7 meals): about 210g porridge (≈ 1 pack of CO-OP 白かゆ), about 105g vegetables (≈ 1 pack of なめらかキューブ), 35–70g protein (≈ 1 pack of 白身魚ほぐし身 60g, or about 70g 絹ごし豆腐).
  - Double everything once on 2 meals a day. Confidence: medium.
- **中期 (7–8 months), 2 meals a day = 14 meals a week.**
  - 全がゆ 50–80g → 700–1,120g a week, roughly 140–225g raw rice.
  - Vegetables and fruit 20–30g → 280–420g a week.
  - Protein per meal: fish or meat 10–15g, **or** tofu 30–40g, **or** 卵黄1 to ⅓ whole egg, **or** dairy 50–70g.
  - Example split (4 fish, 4 tofu, 3 egg, 3 yogurt): fish 40–60g, tofu 120–160g, about 1 egg, yogurt 150–210g.
  - No seasoning.
- **後期 (9–11 months), 3 meals a day = 21 meals a week.**
  - 全がゆ 90g to 軟飯 80g → about 1.7–1.9kg cooked a week.
  - Vegetables and fruit 30–40g → 630–840g a week.
  - Protein per meal: fish or meat 15g, **or** tofu 45g, **or** ½ egg, **or** dairy 80g.
  - Example split (7 fish or meat, 5 tofu, 5 egg, 4 dairy): fish or meat about 105g, tofu about 225g, 2–3 eggs, dairy about 320g.
  - Okinawa leaflet: salt and soy sauce **1g per day in total**.
- **完了期 (12–18 months), 3 meals a day plus 1–2 snacks.**
  - 軟飯 90g to ご飯 80g → about 1.7–1.9kg a week. The Okinawa leaflet prints 軟飯 80g; I used the national guide's 90g.
  - Vegetables and fruit 40–50g → 840–1,050g a week.
  - Protein per meal: fish or meat 15–20g, **or** tofu 50–55g, **or** ½ to ⅔ egg, **or** dairy 100g.
  - Plan snacks separately.
- **Buying for a delivery cycle:** frozen items keep 6 months to 2 years (per the catalogue labels), so the app can buy them in multi-week quantities. Buy fresh local produce and fish in-store for the week, since they are not verifiably in the delivery catalogue.
