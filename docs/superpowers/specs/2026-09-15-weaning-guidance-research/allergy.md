# Japanese guidance on starting allergenic foods (as of 2026-09-15), checked against the app's suggestion logic

Short answer: the current logic matches no Japanese guidance. Japan says "don't delay" allergenic foods. It does not say "offer every labelled allergen as soon as solids start". The only strong early-introduction advice is for egg. The allergen list the app uses has also been out of date since 2024.

**Status of the documents:**
- **食物アレルギー診療ガイドライン2021 (JGFA2021)** is still the latest published edition (2021-11-13). No 2024 or 2025 edition exists.
- **食物アレルギー診療ガイドライン2026（案）** is a draft. Public comment ran 2026-08-10 to 08-26. The draft's schedule table plans publication on 11月28日 (Hamamatsu). I read the full draft text from the e-book viewer's text file. The wording may still change.
- **食物アレルギーの診療の手引き2023** has no newer edition. The PDF was re-issued on 2026-04-10 with the labelling table updated.
- **食物アレルギーの栄養食事指導の手引き2022** was re-issued as a PDF on 2026-04-06.
- **鶏卵アレルギー発症予防に関する提言 (2017)** has not been formally updated. JGFA2021 and the 2026 draft build on it.
- **授乳・離乳の支援ガイド 2019** is still the current version on the こども家庭庁 (CFA) site. I found no revision.

## Q1. What JSPACI and the 2019 weaning guide say

### 1a. When to start weaning, and not delaying allergenic foods
- **JGFA2021 digest, ch.6, 要旨3:**
  - (a) 「乳児に対して食物アレルギー予防のために、離乳食開始時期や食物アレルギーの原因食物となりやすい食物（鶏卵など）の摂取開始を遅らせることは推奨されない。」
  - (b) Delaying the start of weaning, or of common allergens such as egg, is not recommended.
  - (c) https://www.jspaci.jp/guide2021/jgfa2021_6.html
  - (d) 2021. (e) High.
- **JGFA2026 draft, 表6-2 (p.53), recommendations marked ○ = 推奨する:**
  - (a) 「生後5～6か月頃の開始が適当であり、離乳食の開始を遅らせない」 and 「湿疹発症早期から治療を開始し、離乳食開始前には湿疹を寛解させる」
  - Also p.53: 「固形食を生後4か月より前から開始しても食物アレルギー発症を減らすことはできない」
  - (b) Start at 5–6 months, don't delay. Treat eczema early and get it into remission before weaning starts. Starting before 4 months does not help.
  - (c) Notice: https://www.jspaci.jp/news/both/20260810-6369/. Text: https://kk-kyowa-digitalpublishing.meclib.jp/library/books/pubcom26jgfa/book/data/txtsearch.txt
  - (d) Aug 2026, draft. (e) High that this is the draft text.
- **授乳・離乳の支援ガイド2019, p.33:**
  - (a) 「食物アレルギーの発症を心配して、離乳の開始や特定の食物の摂取開始を遅らせても、食物アレルギーの予防効果があるという科学的根拠はないことから、生後５～６か月頃から離乳を始めるように情報提供を行う。」
  - Same page, on eczema: 「子どもに湿疹がある場合や既に食物アレルギーの診断がされている場合…必ず医師の指示に基づいて行うよう情報提供を行うこと。」
  - (b) No evidence that delaying helps; start at 5–6 months. If the baby has eczema or a diagnosed allergy, follow a doctor's instructions.
  - (c) https://www.cfa.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/6790a829-15c7-49d3-9156-9e40e8d9c20c/b0946e59/20230401_policies_boshihoken_junyuu_01.pdf
  - (d) March 2019. (e) High.

### 1b. Egg
**The 2017 recommendation (提言)**
- **Core statement:**
  - (a) 「アトピー性皮膚炎（痒みのある乳児湿疹を含む炎症性の皮膚炎）に罹患した乳児では…医師の管理のもと，生後6か月から鶏卵の微量摂取を開始することを推奨する」 and 「鶏卵の摂取を開始する前に，アトピー性皮膚炎を寛解させることが望ましい」
  - (b) For infants with atopic dermatitis (including itchy infant eczema): start a tiny amount of egg at 6 months under a doctor's management. Clear the eczema first.
  - (c) https://www.jspaci.jp/uploads/2017/06/teigen20170616.pdf, 提言の骨子 (p.1)
  - (d) 2017-06. (e) High.
- **Amounts (表2, p.7), all under a doctor:**
  - Step 1, from 6 months: 50 mg heated whole-egg powder, about 0.2 g heated whole egg (example: about 0.2 g hard-boiled egg white).
  - Step 2, from 9 months: 250 mg powder, about 1.1 g.
  - Check at 12 months: 7 g powder, about half a hard-boiled egg.
  - The trial gave each step daily for 3 months.
- **Not a rigid protocol (p.6–7):**
  - (a) 「すべての乳児に画一的に当てはめることを本提言は推奨していない．重要なのは，生後6か月頃から微量の鶏卵摂取を開始する，という点である」
  - Once the tiny amount is tolerated: 「従来どおり『授乳・離乳の支援ガイド』に準拠して…進めてよい」
- **Infants without eczema (p.7–8):**
  - (a) 「湿疹を有さない乳児の場合，鶏卵開始時期が鶏卵アレルギーの発症に影響を与えるというエビデンスは現在のところ存在しない」
  - (b) For these babies, just follow the weaning guide.
  - Egg yolk (p.7): hard-boiled yolk is low risk. A yolk separated 1 hour after boiling, ≤1/3 yolk, carries no more ovomucoid than 0.2 g of egg white.
- **Explainer for doctors (JSPACI, 2017-10-12):**
  - (a) Starting form: 「固ゆで卵黄」や「できる限りよく加熱したいり卵」を使い、「ごはん粒の10分の1程度の大きさ」
  - (a) Warning: 「摂らせる時機が早すぎる、非加熱鶏卵や加熱鶏卵でも摂取量が多い場合には逆効果になることにも留意すべき」
  - (b) Start with a piece about a tenth of a rice grain. Starting too early, or giving too much (even cooked), can backfire.
  - (c) https://www.jspaci.jp/uploads/2017/10/97d5be77f6c2e7973e69a30a80a2a84b-1.pdf
  - (e) High.

**Changes since 2017**
The recommendation has moved from "eczema infants only" towards "all infants", and from yolk towards whole egg:

| Source | Wording on egg | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| JGFA2021 表6-4 | 「生後5～6か月から加熱卵黄を摂取開始してよい」 | Heated yolk, "may" |
| 手引き2023 表7 (p.11) | 「生後5〜6か月から加熱鶏卵を微量もしくは少量から摂取開始してよい」 | Heated egg, "may"; [PDF](https://www.foodallergy.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/FAmanual2023_260410.pdf) |
| JGFA2026 draft 表6-2 | 「○ 生後5～6か月頃から加熱卵を摂取開始する」 | Heated egg, now "recommended" |

- The draft also says (p.54): eczema infants, after proper eczema treatment, 「生後6か月からごく少量の加熱鶏卵を段階的に導入」.
- **国立成育医療研究センター (NCCHD) egg page, dated 2023-10-12:**
  - (a) 「卵黄から開始することにこだわらず」
  - Schedule: from 6–8 months 「ゆで卵白1gの1/8個(0.125g)を1日1回毎日」; from 9–11 months 0.75 g daily.
  - Aimed at high-risk infants with eczema, with the doctor's agreement.
  - (c) https://www.ncchd.go.jp/hospital/about/section/allergy/keiran_sessyu.html
  - (e) High.
- **Weaning guide sequence (p.31, table p.34):**
  - 5–6 months: 「つぶしがゆから始める。すりつぶした野菜等も試してみる。慣れてきたら、つぶした豆腐・白身魚・卵黄等を試してみる。」
  - Amounts per meal: 7–8 months 卵黄1～全卵1/3; 9–11 months 全卵1/2; 12–18 months 全卵1/2～2/3.
  - 「卵は卵黄から全卵へと進めていく」
  - The guide gives no number of days before yolk.
- **Two cautions in the 2026 draft:**
  - p.82: 「卵ボーロは十分に焼成されていたとしてもアレルゲン性は低下しておらず」 (tamago bolo is still allergenic despite baking).
  - ch.16: egg-yolk FPIES (a delayed, non-IgE reaction) is rising in Japan. Symptom is repeated vomiting 1–4 hours after eating.

### 1c. Milk
- **SPADE trial** (Sakihara et al., JACI 2021;147:224-32; newborns from 4 hospitals in Okinawa):
  - Design: at least 10 mL of standard formula daily between 1 and 2 months of age, versus avoiding it.
  - Result: cow's milk allergy at 6 months was 0.8% vs 6.8%.
  - https://www.jacionline.org/article/S0091-6749(20)31225-2/fulltext. (e) High.
- **手引き2023 表7:**
  - Formula only in the first 3 days of life (≥5 mL/day) was linked to more milk allergy at 1 year.
  - 「生後1か月以降に普通ミルクを1日10mL以上追加すると、その後の牛乳アレルギー発症が抑制された」
- **JGFA2026 draft:**
  - 表6-2 ○: 「新生児期に普通調製乳を摂取する場合は、生後1か月以降も少量でも継続させる」
  - p.52: 「意図的に摂取中の食物タンパクを長期間除去しないほうがよい」 (don't deliberately stop a food protein the baby is already getting).
- **Weaning guide:** dairy 50–70 g per meal at 7–8 months. Cow's milk as a drink 「１歳を過ぎてからが望ましい」 (because of iron).
- **Where sources differ:** the ERCA table of foreign guidelines shows that EAACI 2021 (avoid standard formula in week 1) and ESPGHAN 2024 take different positions.

### 1d. Wheat and soy
- **2017 recommendation, p.8:**
  - (a) 「牛乳や小麦など、他の食品を早期摂取する効果も、現時点では確認されていない」
  - The doctor explainer adds that delaying weaning in general clearly does not help.
  - (e) High.
- JGFA2026 draft 表6-2 has no wheat or soy item.
- Weaning guide: tofu comes after porridge and vegetables at 5–6 months. There is no specific age for wheat.
- Wheat is the #3 new-onset cause at age 0 (13.1%).

### 1e. Peanut and tree nuts: Japan does not recommend LEAP-style early introduction
- **JGFA2021:** only cites the international consensus and does not recommend early peanut. Source: the JECS analysis (J Epidemiol 2024), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11167260/.
  - Its conclusion: "In countries with a low prevalence of PA, the effect of infantile peanut introduction on PA prevention was unclear." (e) High.
- **JGFA2026 draft, p.54:**
  - (a) 「少なくともピーナッツアレルギーの有病率が高い国・地域では、乳児期に早期にピーナッツの摂取を開始するほうがよいというのが国際的コンセンサスである。なお、ナッツ類に関しては鶏卵・ピーナッツほどの確固たるエビデンスはなく…」
  - (a) 「食物アレルギーをすでに発症している可能性がある児に原因食物を摂取させることは危険な行為であるため…自宅ではなく医療機関での食物経口負荷試験…」
  - (b) Early peanut is the consensus only for high-prevalence regions. Tree-nut evidence is weaker than for egg or peanut. If an allergy may already exist, the first exposure should be an oral food challenge (a supervised test feed) in hospital, not at home.
  - (e) High that this is the draft text.
- **Sakihara review** (アレルギー 73(3):260-267, 2024, p.265):
  - (a) 「現時点で，日本でピーナッツの早期摂取を推奨する十分なエビデンスはない」
  - (a) 「日本で急激に増加している木の実類アレルギーの早期摂取の有効性を実証した報告はまだない」; aspiration risk must be considered.
  - https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/arerugi/73/3/73_260/_pdf/-char/ja. (e) High.
- **Okinawa**, from an ERCA column reporting Sakihara's talk at JSPACI, Nov 2024:
  - (a) 「日本はピーナッツアレルギーの有病率が0.2％と低い国なので、全体ではなく、沖縄県などの有病率が高い地域に限定して、かつ湿疹のある子どもに対して早期摂取の検討の余地があるかもしれません」
  - https://www.erca.go.jp/yobou/zensoku/sukoyaka/column/202507_1/. (e) Medium: expert opinion.
  - Okinawa Medical Association column (2023-07-27): at Nakagami Hospital, 2017–2021, peanut was the #1 cause, about a quarter of food-allergy cases. Single centre, so medium confidence.
- **NCCHD pilot** (Harama et al., Nutrients 2024;16:1578):
  - 34 infants with eczema, specialist-guided, started egg, milk, wheat, peanut, walnut and cashew from about 6 months as powder or smooth paste. No choking.
  - (a) 「今回の結果はアレルギーの発症を防ぐ効果を示したものではありません…必ずアレルギー専門の医師の指導の下で」 (this shows feasibility, not prevention; do it only under an allergy specialist).
  - https://www.ncchd.go.jp/press/2024/0613.html. (e) High.
- **Choking (消費者庁):** 「硬くてかみ砕く必要のある豆やナッツ類は、5歳以下の子どもには食べさせないで」. https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/consumer_safety/caution/caution_047/
- **Other 2025–26 statements:** I found no JSPACI or JSA statement recommending walnut or cashew early introduction.
  - Kitamura (日本小児科学会雑誌 130(3):467-475, 2026) stresses that the danger is an unexpected first ingestion in early childhood.
  - The JGFA2026 draft puts walnut first-reaction median age at 3.9 years.

### 1f. Fish roe (いくら), shrimp and crab, buckwheat (そば)
- **No official age anywhere.** The weaning guide lists none of them. 母子栄養協会 notes: 「『授乳・離乳の支援ガイド』でも、そばの開始時期は具体的に示されていない」 ([source](https://boshieiyou.org/howto_soba/)). (e) High.
- **Epidemiology (JGFA2026 draft ch.12):**
  - いくら is the #3 new-onset cause at 1–2 years and #2 at 3–6 years.
  - Crustacean allergy typically starts from school age.
  - Buckwheat allergy 「幼児期に即時型症状で発症することが多い」.
- **Common practice (not guidelines):**
  - いくら from about 3 years (it is raw, salty, and a top cause at 1–6 years).
  - そば from about 1 to 1.5 years.
  - Shrimp and crab from 1 year or later. The Chibana clinic in Okinawa suggests ground dried sakura shrimp.
  - The CFA weaning leaflet bans raw meat, fish and egg during weaning.
  - (e) Medium-low: these are conventions.

### 1g. How to introduce a new (allergenic) food
- **Amount:**
  - Weaning guide p.31: 「新しい食品を始める時には離乳食用のスプーンで１さじずつ与え、子どもの様子をみながら量を増やしていく」
  - CFA schedule leaflet: 「新しい食材は、ひとさじ量で試し、食物アレルギーの反応や消化の状態を見ながら少しずつ進めていきましょう」
  - For egg: a tenth of a rice grain (JSPACI explainer) or 0.125 g egg white (NCCHD).
  - Chibana clinic: high-risk foods from 「米１粒程度」or「耳かき１杯程度」; rice and vegetables 「小さじ1杯前後からでも良い」. That is one clinic, so medium confidence.
- **Time of day:**
  - 栄養食事指導の手引き2022: 「初めての食物を与えるときは、患者の体調のよいときに、新鮮な食材を、充分に加熱し、少量から与える。平日の昼間に与えると、症状が出た場合に医師の診察を受けやすい。」 ([PDF](https://www.foodallergy.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/nutritionalmanual2022-20260406.pdf)). (e) High.
  - JSPACI patient explainer: 「症状がでたらすぐ受診できる時間に摂取してください」.
- **Days at the same amount before increasing:** no Japanese guideline gives a number.
  - PETIT held each step for 3 months, but that was a research protocol.
  - Chibana clinic: 「1-2日ずつ様子をみながら少しずつ増量」 (medium confidence).
- **One new food at a time:** implied by the "one spoonful" rule. ERCA: 「離乳食はもともと1品ずつ開始していくのが原則」.
- **Eczema:** see the weaning guide and draft 表6-2 quotes in 1a. Refer to a specialist 「多抗原(3抗原以上)感作…離乳食開始までに」 (JGFA2026 draft ch.8; 手引き2023).
- **Nursery:** 保育所におけるアレルギー対応ガイドライン2019: 「家庭において可能であれば２回以上、保育所で提供する量程度、もしくはそれ以上の量を食べて何ら症状が誘発されないことを確認した上で」. (e) High.

### 1h. Keeping an introduced allergen in the diet
- **No Japanese guideline sets a frequency.**
- Daily in practice or trials: PETIT and NCCHD (「1日1回毎日」); formula ≥10 mL a day (SPADE, 手引き2023).
- JGFA2026 draft p.54: 「乳児期に少量から定期的・持続的に摂取することによる経口耐性誘導」 (regular, ongoing small intake builds tolerance).
- **Sakihara 2024, relaying the Canadian CSACI 2023 statement:**
  - 「早期開始かつ定期的に摂取する（少なくとも週1回以上）」
  - 「単回もしくは低頻度の摂取は、むしろ食物アレルギー発症のリスクとなる可能性がある」
  - 「定期的な摂取が難しいと予想される場合は、開始せずに除去のままがいいかもしれない」
  - (b) At least weekly. A one-off or occasional exposure may raise risk. If regular intake is unlikely, it may be better not to start.
- ERCA 2025 (Sakihara): 「週１、２回以上の定期的な摂取を継続する必要」.
- (e) Medium: this is expert opinion and imported guidance.

## Q4. Days between new foods
- **Japan gives no number of days.**
  - The weaning guide says one spoonful, then increase while watching (p.31).
  - The CFA leaflet says 「ひとさじ量で試し…見ながら少しずつ」.
  - (e) High.
- **Japan does separate low-risk from high-risk foods, but by amount and care, not by days:**
  - 栄養食事指導の手引き2022: 「離乳食開始時に利用しやすい米、野菜類（大根、人参、カボチャ、サツマイモ など）が原因食物となることは少ない」
  - Chibana clinic: rice and vegetables can start at about 1 teaspoon; high-risk foods at a rice grain or ear-pick amount.
- **The "3 days" rule comes from the US.** AAP HealthyChildren (updated 2026-06-23): "Introduce one 'single-ingredient' new food from any food group every 3 to 5 days." A flat 3 days is just the low end of that rule.
  - It is contested. Samady, Gupta et al. (JAMA Netw Open 2020;3:e2013070): "From the perspective of food allergy detection, there is no reason to wait three to five days". Waiting also reduces diet diversity and can delay peanut.
- **Other countries:**
  - ASCIA 2026 (Australia): "only introduce one new allergen at the same meal". No day gap.
  - NHS (UK, reviewed 2024-10): "one at a time". No day gap.
- **Why days aren't needed to spot a reaction:** IgE reactions show within 2 hours (JGFA2026 draft ch.2 definition of 即時型). FPIES shows as vomiting 1–4 hours after eating.

## Implications for the recommendation engine
Each rule names its source. "Heuristic" means no guideline sets that value.

1. **Gate on weaning stage, not just "not yet tried".**
   - Stage 1 order: つぶしがゆ → mashed vegetables → (「慣れてきたら」) tofu, white fish, hard-boiled yolk.
   - The guide sets no day count. A threshold such as "porridge plus ≥3–4 vegetables eaten several times" is a heuristic.
   - Source: 授乳・離乳の支援ガイド2019, p.31 and p.34.
2. **Egg is the one allergen to push early.**
   - From 5–6 months, once porridge, vegetables and tofu are established, suggest well-cooked egg: hard-boiled yolk at a tiny amount (a tenth of a rice grain up to an ear-pick).
   - Move to whole egg once yolk is tolerated. Stage targets: 7–8 months 卵黄1～全卵1/3; 9–11 months 全卵1/2; 12–18 months 全卵1/2～2/3.
   - Never raw or soft egg. Don't treat tamago bolo as a low-allergen step.
   - Sources: JGFA2021 表6-4; 手引き2023 表7; JGFA2026 draft 表6-2 and p.82; weaning guide p.34; CFA leaflet.
3. **Eczema gate.** If eczema, known sensitisation or a previous reaction is recorded, stop auto-suggesting allergens and show "discuss with your doctor first; get the eczema under control before weaning". The app currently doesn't capture eczema status.
   - Sources: 2017 提言; weaning guide p.33; JGFA2026 draft 表6-2 and p.54.
4. **Milk.** Yogurt and low-salt cheese from 7–8 months (50–70 g); cow's milk as a drink only after 1 year. If the baby already has standard formula, count milk as introduced and keep it regular.
   - Sources: weaning guide p.31 and p.34; SPADE; JGFA2026 draft 表6-2.
5. **Wheat and soy.** Introduce in the normal sequence (tofu in stage 1, wheat such as udon or bread porridge from about stage 2). No priority boost, but don't hold them back.
   - Sources: 2017 提言 p.8; weaning guide.
6. **Peanut and tree nuts: no LEAP-based top score.**
   - Only suggest if: core foods (egg, milk, wheat, soy) are established; the form is smooth paste or powder only, never whole or chopped nuts (under 6); and the family commits to at least weekly intake.
   - With eczema: only under an allergist.
   - For Okinawa, show a "discuss with your pediatrician" note. Don't auto-push.
   - Sources: JGFA2026 draft p.54; Sakihara 2024 p.265; ERCA 2025; NCCHD 2024; CAA choking warning.
7. **いくら, shrimp, crab, そば: exclude from weaning-stage suggestions.**
   - Earliest heuristic: shrimp and crab from about 1 year (cooked, minced, tiny); そば 1–1.5 years; いくら about 3 years.
   - Label these as convention, not guideline. Basis: no age in the weaning guide; いくら is a top new-onset cause at 1–6 years; raw foods are banned during weaning.
8. **First exposure to any allergen:** weekday daytime, baby well, tiny amount, watch at least 2 hours, and be aware of vomiting 1–4 hours later (egg yolk).
   - Sources: 栄養食事指導の手引き2022; JSPACI explainer; JGFA2026 draft ch.16.
9. **Spacing.**
   - At most one new major allergen per day.
   - Low-risk vegetables and grains: no fixed gap (another new vegetable the next day is fine).
   - Increase an allergen in steps after 1–2 uneventful days at the same amount. This is a heuristic from one clinic; PETIT stepped up only every 3 months.
   - Sources: weaning guide; ASCIA 2026; Chibana clinic; Samady 2020.
10. **Maintenance.** Once an allergen is tolerated, re-suggest it at least weekly (more often for egg and milk) and flag lapses. Don't start an allergen the family won't keep giving.
    - Sources: Sakihara 2024 citing CSACI; ASCIA 2026 ("at least once a week"); JGFA2026 draft p.54.
11. **Nursery.** Mark foods as "nursery-ready" only after ≥2 home exposures at nursery-sized portions.
    - Source: 保育所におけるアレルギー対応ガイドライン2019.
12. **Allergen list.** Replace it with the 9 + 20 lists above as of 2026-04-01. Drop matsutake; add pistachio and macadamia; make cashew mandatory. Don't use "mandatory" as a priority signal for introduction.

## Where the current implementation contradicts Japanese guidance
1. **Egg, wheat, dairy and peanut paste as top picks one week into solids.** At that point the guide expects only porridge and some vegetables. JSPACI explicitly warns that starting too early or giving too much 「逆効果になる」. This is the opposite of the guidance.
2. **"LEAP/PETIT" misapplied:**
   - LEAP covers peanut in high-prevalence countries. JGFA2021 doesn't endorse it for Japan, the 2026 draft hedges, and Sakihara says 「推奨する十分なエビデンスはない」.
   - PETIT covers egg only, in eczema infants, after remission, under a doctor, as a daily micro-dose from 6 months. JSPACI states milk and wheat benefits are 「確認されていない」.
3. **Buckwheat, shrimp and crab ranked high during weaning.** No Japanese source supports weaning-age introduction.
4. **Walnut, and now cashew, prioritised.** There is no Japanese early-introduction recommendation, whole nuts are banned under 6 (CAA), and these nuts cause shock at high rates (cashew 13.3%, macadamia 18.8%).
5. **Flat 3 days for every food, carrot included.** This is not Japanese. It's the US AAP lower bound, and it's contested. It slows diet diversity for no detection benefit.
6. **The allergen list is out of date:**
   - Matsutake was removed in 2024-03.
   - Cashew has been mandatory since 2026-04-01.
   - Pistachio is missing. Check macadamia too.
7. **No eczema input.** Every Japanese source treats eczema as the key risk modifier.
8. **No maintenance logic.** Introduce-once-then-forget goes against the "regular, ongoing intake" message.
9. **Seed-list details:**
   - Tamago bolo at phase 3 must not count as a gentle egg step (JGFA2026 draft p.82).
   - Peanut butter at phase 2 and ground walnut or cashew at phase 3 are not Japanese-guideline placements.

## Where Japan differs from US/UK/Australia, and where sources disagree
- **Peanut:**
  - US NIAID 2017: severe eczema or egg allergy → evaluate, then 4–6 months; mild–moderate eczema → around 6 months ([PDF](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/sites/default/files/addendum-peanut-allergy-prevention-guidelines.pdf)).
  - ASCIA 2026: all infants, soon after solids start.
  - NHS: from around 6 months, one at a time.
  - Japan: neutral. Don't delay, but no recommendation.
- **Tree nuts:** ASCIA 2026 says introduce in the first year, prioritising foods the family eats. Japan says the evidence isn't established, and CAA's choking rule bans whole nuts under 6.
- **Egg:** everyone agrees on early. Japan's model is a heated micro-dose stepped up gradually.
- **Spacing:** US AAP says 3–5 days. Australia and the UK say one allergen per meal or one at a time. Japan says one spoonful and watch, with no days.
- **Keeping it up:** ASCIA 2026 says at least weekly. Japan has only expert opinion (≥1–2 times a week).
- **Disagreements inside Japan:**
  - **Yolk first or whole egg:** the weaning guide and JGFA2021 say heated yolk. PETIT, NCCHD and the 2026 draft ("加熱卵", ○) favour whole egg or white.
  - **Egg for non-eczema infants:** 2017 said there was no evidence. JGFA2021, the 2026 draft and Sakihara now say start egg protein at 5–6 months for all.
  - **Nuts:** the NCCHD pilot starts peanut and nuts at 6 months in eczema infants, while JGFA and Sakihara stay cautious. Sakihara suggests an Okinawa/eczema exception may be worth considering.
  - **Ages for いくら, そば, shrimp:** vary between sources that aren't guidelines.
